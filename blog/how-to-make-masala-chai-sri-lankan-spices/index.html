How to Make Masala Chai with Sri Lankan Spices

A cup of masala tea made with Sri Lankan cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, ginger, nutmeg, fennel and pepper, using Leora Naturale Chai Masala.

What is chai masala?

Chai masala is a ground spice blend for masala tea. Leora Naturale Chai Masala uses seven Sri Lankan spices: cinnamon (kurundu), cardamom (karadamungu), cloves (karabu neti), ginger (inguru), nutmeg (sadikka), fennel (maduru) and black pepper (gammiris). It is a muddy brown powder with no tea leaves, so you add it to your own tea.

How do you make masala chai?

  1. Bring one cup of water to the boil with one teaspoon of black tea or one tea bag.
  2. Add a small pinch of Chai Masala.
  3. Pour in half a cup of milk and simmer for two to three minutes.
  4. Sweeten with sugar or jaggery, strain and serve hot.

Use more spice for a stronger cup, less for a gentle one.

Why Sri Lankan spices?

Sri Lanka is famous for true cinnamon, and its cardamom, cloves, nutmeg and pepper are grown on the same island. That is the flavour of a Sri Lankan kitchen in every cup.

Other ways to use it

  • Stir a pinch into hot milk before bed.
  • Add it to cake, cookie or pancake batter.
  • Sprinkle it over porridge.

How should I store it?

Keep the pouch sealed in a cool, dry place away from sunlight so the spices keep their aroma.
