FAQ

Common Questions

What is Heen Bovitiya tea?

Heen Bovitiya tea is a traditional Sri Lankan herbal tea made from Osbeckia octandra leaves. Leora Naturale packs it in Sri Lanka in boxes of 25 or 15 caffeine-free tea bags.

How do I brew it?

Steep one bag in freshly boiled water for five to seven minutes. Enjoy it plain or with a little honey.

What pack sizes are there?

25 tea bags for Rs 875.00 or 15 tea bags for Rs 525.00.

Does it contain caffeine?

No. Heen Bovitiya is a herb, not tea from the Camellia sinensis plant.
