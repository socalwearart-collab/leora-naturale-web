FAQ

Common Questions

What is Butterfly Pea Flower?

Butterfly pea (Clitoria ternatea), called nil katarolu in Sinhala and sangu poo in Tamil, is a flower that brews into a vivid blue, caffeine-free herbal drink.

How do I brew it?

Steep the flowers in warm water for 3 to 5 minutes.

Can I use it as a food colour?

Yes. Use the blue brew to colour string hoppers, rice and desserts naturally.

What is in the pack?

100% dried butterfly pea flower, 10 g.
