What Is Heen Bovitiya Tea and How Do You Brew It?

Heen Bovitiya is a traditional Sri Lankan herbal tea. Here is what it is, how to brew a cup at home, and where to buy it abroad from Leora Naturale.

What is Heen Bovitiya tea?

Heen Bovitiya tea is a traditional Sri Lankan herbal tea made from the leaves of Osbeckia octandra, a flowering shrub that grows across the island. It is caffeine-free and is not related to black or green tea. Leora Naturale packs it in Sri Lanka in boxes of 25 or 15 tea bags and ships it to Sri Lankan families abroad.

Where does it come from?

Heen Bovitiya grows wild in Sri Lanka and has been brewed in village kitchens for generations. For many families it was the cup that was always on the stove: simple, earthy and tied to memories of home. That is why so many Sri Lankans overseas go looking for it.

How do you brew Heen Bovitiya tea?

Each Leora Naturale pack holds 25 or 15 tea bags, so brewing takes no measuring.

  1. Bring fresh water to the boil.
  2. Put one tea bag in a cup.
  3. Pour the hot water over the bag.
  4. Steep for five to seven minutes. Leave it longer for a stronger cup.
  5. Remove the bag and enjoy it warm.

If the brewing guide printed on your pack differs, follow the pack.

How does it taste?

Expect a light, floral, slightly earthy flavour. Many people drink it plain. Others add a little honey, jaggery or a squeeze of lime, the way it is often served at home.

What goes well with it?

Heen Bovitiya is a quiet evening cup. On other days, try its sister products: Heen Bovitiya powder stirred into warm water, or a bright blue cup of butterfly pea flower tea.

Is it easy to keep and ship abroad?

Yes. The herb is dried, so the tea is light, compact and needs no refrigeration. Keep the pouch sealed in a cool, dry place and it travels well to Australia, Canada, the Middle East and Russia.
